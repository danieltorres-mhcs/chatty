Chatty

A plain, invite-only chat room where everyone is pseudonymous.

What Chatty is

Chatty is a small web chat. You pick a username (no real name or email needed), join with an invite code, and talk in a single shared room. Messages are kept for two months and then deleted automatically.

What you can do

  • Chat in real time, with markdown, @mentions, slash commands, muting and your own colors and settings.
  • Sign in with a password, a passkey, or a connected Google account.
  • Browse as a guest without an account: guests can read today’s messages but cannot post.
  • Change the language, text size, font and contrast to suit you.

How to join

New accounts need an invite code. You can request one from the sign-up page by telling us why you want to join and how to reach you.

About Google sign-in

Connecting a Google account is optional. It is used only to let you sign in to your existing Chatty account. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored.
