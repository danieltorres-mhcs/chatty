Privacy policy

Chatty · Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains what Chatty collects, why, and the choices you have. Chatty is run by Dani (“we”). You can reach us at at our e-mail. .

What we collect

  • Account: your username, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), the time you joined, and your sign-in sessions. We do not ask for your real email. Our login system stores a placeholder address made from your username.
  • Messages: what you write, who wrote it, when, and whether it was edited. If you delete a message, the original text is kept privately so the operator can handle abuse.
  • Profile and settings saved to your account: your bio, name color, list of muted users, name-muting level, welcome-message choice and last login time.
  • Passkeys: if you add one, we store its public credential data. Your fingerprint, face or device PIN never leaves your device.
  • Recovery code: we store a fingerprint (hash) of it to check it, and a copy encrypted with your password so you can view it again. We cannot read it.
  • Invites and requests: the invite code used to join and, if you request an invite, your answers (why you want to join, who you are, how to contact you).
  • Sign-up records: your IP address and a random device ID when you create an account, to fight spam and abuse. For temporary invite codes we also keep scrambled (hashed) versions to enforce a limit of 4 accounts per device and network.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you connect a Google account, we receive its account identifier, email address and basic profile information through our login provider.
  • On your device: your session, language, display settings and a random device ID are saved in your browser’s local storage. Chatty does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

To run the chat, sign you in, keep your settings, prevent abuse and respond to invite requests. We do not sell your data, show ads, build advertising profiles or use your data to train AI.

Google user data

If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Chatty only requests your basic profile (name, email address and account identifier). We use it solely to link that Google account to your Chatty account so you can sign in. We do not show it to other users, share it with anyone except our login provider (Supabase), or use it for advertising. Chatty’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect Google at any time in Settings > Security.

Who can see what

  • Your username, messages, bio and name color are visible to everyone in the chat.
  • Guests (no account) can read messages from the current day but cannot post.
  • The operator can see sign-up records, invite requests and deleted messages.

Who we share data with

Only the services needed to run Chatty: Supabase (database and login), GitHub (web hosting), jsDelivr (open-source libraries and optional fonts) and Google (only if you use Google sign-in). They process data on our behalf under their own policies. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Messages: automatically deleted after 60 days.
  • Sign-up and limit records and deleted-message copies: up to 60 days.
  • Invite codes: up to 30 days.
  • Account data: until you delete your account.

Your choices

You can delete your account, messages, passkeys and recovery code yourself in Settings > Account > Delete my account. All of it will be deleted. You can also email us to ask what we hold about you or to have it removed.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Age restrictions

Chatty is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
